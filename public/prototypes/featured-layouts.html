Featured layouts

Four ways to show the Featured / Most recent pick without cropping, whatever the image's shape. Flip Featured ↔ Most recent in each to see how it handles a change of aspect ratio, and use the pair picker to stress-test.

Test pair
A

Stage

Fixed height, image contained, letterbox in the image's own edge colour (sampled at build time). Mockups on cream melt into the frame; a dark render gets a dark stage. Label in a frosted chip instead of a scrim. Height never changes, so the cross-fade stays still.

Read case study
B

Blur backdrop

Fixed height, image contained over a blurred, enlarged copy of itself. Works for anything, including busy photos, and always fills the frame. Louder than A; reads more "media player" than "editorial".

Read case study
C

Editorial split

Text column + media at a fixed height; the media box takes the image's own ratio. Room for a one-line summary and a clear CTA, and the photo is never covered by text. A tall image gives the text more room; a wide one squeezes it to its minimum. Stacks on mobile.

Read case study
D

Justified pair

No cycler: both picks in one row, widths proportional to their ratios, height solved to fill the row. Zero cropping, zero letterbox, both visible at once, captions underneath. Height varies with the pair (it's a different shape per pair, not per click).
