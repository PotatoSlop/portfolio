## Context

NUHire is a hiring simulation run live in Khoury CS1210, Intro to Co-op. It replaces a pen-and-paper activity called "employer for a day". Students play the employer in groups of three to five: they read a job description, review resumes on a timer, agree on candidates to interview, watch recorded interviews, and extend one offer. A professor runs the room in real time, assigning groups, sending curveballs mid-interview, and accepting or rejecting each offer.

The learning goal is simple: understand what a hiring manager actually looks for in a resume.

<Callout>How can we scale this activity cleanly via software?</Callout>

## Mapping the activity

Before any screens, I mapped who owns what. The professor creates and manages classes and groups and starts every session; students are invited into a class, sorted into a group, and brought into the activity together. Each session produces a summary for everyone.

<Figure src="/Assets/design/uiux/nuhire/relationships.webp" alt="Relationship map: the professor invites students, creates classes and groups, and starts sessions, which produce summaries" ratio="1880 / 1620" caption="User relationships: who creates, joins and starts what." />

From there, each role got its own flow. Students move from their class into a group or a session, wait until the professor lets them in, then work through the job description, résumés and notes before approving or rejecting candidates and landing on a personal, group and class summary. The professor's side runs from the classes hub through inviting and grouping students, a live overview where steps are opened and locked, and a debrief where they decide what students get to see.
